What HelpU surfaces

The demo can surface supported documentation opportunities such as respiratory failure clarification, heart failure specificity, and anemia documentation.

Why timing matters

The value is timing: the physician sees the clarification while the note is still being signed, reducing downstream rework.

Clinical guardrail

HelpU does not diagnose, auto-edit, or override the clinician. It presents clarification cards for physician judgment.

Demo Rules
