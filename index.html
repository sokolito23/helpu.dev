How It Works

Built for the moment of sign.

HelpU is EHR-embedded by design and brings high-confidence documentation clarification into physician workflow at note sign.

01 -

Note is written

The physician completes the note as usual with no extra documentation step added during the encounter.

02 -

Sign is clicked

At note sign, HelpU evaluates the current documentation for high-confidence clarification opportunities.

03 -

Review and decide

The physician reviews optional, non-leading prompts and decides whether to accept or ignore them. HelpU never auto-edits the note.
